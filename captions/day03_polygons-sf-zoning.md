#30DayMapChallenge Day 3 · Polygons 🏘️

Outside parks and public land, half of San Francisco is zoned for one- or two-family houses. Apartment zones cover about 15%.

Every polygon here is a zoning district from the city's own map, sorted by what you're allowed to build. The blue sweep across the west and south is what most people never see from downtown. This is base zoning only, so state law and special programs can allow more.
