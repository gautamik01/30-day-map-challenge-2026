#30DayMapChallenge Day 3: Polygons 🏘️

Picture San Francisco and you probably see a dense city of towers.

Its zoning map tells a different story. Every block here is colored by what the city allows you to build on it. Outside parks and public land, half of San Francisco is zoned for one- or two-family houses.

What would your city's zoning map reveal?
