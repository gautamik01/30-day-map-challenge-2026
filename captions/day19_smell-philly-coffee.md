## LinkedIn post

#30DayMapChallenge Day 19 · Smell ☕

Philly has 259 coffee spots that aren't Starbucks, and 30 that are. That's almost 9 for every Starbucks.

But coffee smell doesn't travel far. Only 13% of the city's street length is within 250 m of a coffee spot, about a three-minute walk. Most of the aroma piles up in Center City, where one corner Saxbys has 11 other coffee spots within that same 250 m.

I drew every café as a little cloud drifting out over the streets. The smell is a drawing, not a measurement, but the gaps are real.

What's the Philly café you'd happily walk 20 minutes for?
