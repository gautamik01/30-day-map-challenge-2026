## LinkedIn post

#30DayMapChallenge Day 20 · Hexagons 💧

Where does AI get its water? I went looking for the source and found the river basin first.

30 of the 77 big AI campuses in the US (39%) sit in basins under high or extremely high water stress. That means local users already take 40% or more of the water that's available there. Those 30 campuses will draw 16.9 GW at full build, 39% of all the AI power being built.

The surprise is underground. Only 3 campuses sit over groundwater that's dropping fast, and two of them are in Wisconsin.

A basin isn't an intake pipe, so here's the real question: which of these campuses will cool with water, and which with air?

Data: WRI Aqueduct 4.0 · Epoch AI Frontier Data Centers
