## LinkedIn post

#30DayMapChallenge Day 15 · Inside out 🌊

What if the ocean floor were a mountain range?

I took the seafloor under Monterey Bay and turned it over, so every metre of depth becomes a metre of height. The land stays exactly where it is.

89% of this frame is underwater. Its deepest point sits 3,279 m below the surface. Flipped, it would stand almost three times taller than the highest hill on the coast around it. And that long branching ridge running out to sea is Monterey Canyon, upside down.

We plan cities right up to the waterline and almost never think about the landscape that keeps going underneath. What's the most surprising thing you know about the ground under the sea?

Data: NOAA NCEI Coastal Relief Model · height exaggerated 5x
