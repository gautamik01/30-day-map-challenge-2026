#30DayMapChallenge Day 7 · 10 minute map 🌳

Philadelphia passes the 10-minute walk test. The Trust for Public Land says 97% of residents live that close to a park. So I made the park bigger.

In the city's most heat-vulnerable tracts, 98% of streets are within a 10-minute walk of a park. Ask for a park of 10 acres and that falls to 32%, against 47% in the rest of the city. Ten acres is my cutoff, not a cooling standard. These are shares of street length, not people.

How big does a park have to be before it counts?
