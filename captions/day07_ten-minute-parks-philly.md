#30DayMapChallenge Day 7: 10 Minute Map 🌳

Cities are often judged by a simple test: can you walk to a park in 10 minutes? Philadelphia passes. So I made the park bigger.

In its most heat-vulnerable neighborhoods, 98% of streets are within 10 minutes of a park. Only 32% reach one of 10 acres.

Ten acres is my line. Where's yours?
